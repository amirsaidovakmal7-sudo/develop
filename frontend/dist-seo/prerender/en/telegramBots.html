Services · Telegram bots

Bots Telegram bot development for business

I develop custom Telegram bots that work for your manager around the clock: they take requests, book clients, handle orders and payments, answer common questions and pass data to your CRM or a spreadsheet. Each bot is built around your process — from the conversation flow to launch on a server.

Discuss a project→
01·In short

Why a business needs a Telegram bot

Your clients are already in Telegram, and messaging a bot is easier for them than calling or filling in a form on a website. A bot replies instantly at any hour, asks the right questions and hands a complete request to your manager. Your staff stop spending time on the same answers, and enquiries don't get lost in personal chats.

02·Formats

Bots I build

  • 01

    Lead capture bot

    Collects the name, phone number and request and sends it straight to your manager in the staff group.

  • 02

    Booking bot

    Shows the schedule, books the client and sends reminders about the visit or lesson on its own.

  • 03

    Ordering bot

    A menu or catalogue, a cart, a delivery address and payment — for a café, a delivery service or a shop.

  • 04

    Team assistant

    Broadcasts, reminders, schedules and reports for your staff or students.
