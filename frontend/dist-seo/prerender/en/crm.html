Services · CRM

CRM Custom CRM development for your business

I develop CRM systems around the processes of a specific company — instead of spreadsheets, notebooks and chats. All clients, deals and tasks live in one place, requests from your website and Telegram bot land there automatically, and the owner sees sales reports.

01·In short

What a CRM is, in plain words

A CRM is a system that holds all your work with clients: who got in touch, what was agreed, what stage a deal is at and which employee is responsible for it. A custom CRM follows your sales process instead of forcing you into someone else’s template, so it suits a small business and a company with several departments alike.

02·Formats

What you can do in a CRM

  • 01

    Sales pipeline

    Every deal moves through clear stages, and you can see at which step clients drop off.

  • 02

    Tasks and roles

    Employees get tasks and see only what they need for their work.

  • 03

    Reports

    Requests, sales and manager performance in numbers, without merging spreadsheets by hand.

  • 04

    Integrations

    Links to your website, Telegram bot, online payments and Google Sheets.

03·Scope

What's included in CRM development

  • Client base and request history
  • Sales pipeline and deal stages
  • Tasks for your team
  • Roles and access levels
  • Reports and analytics
  • Links to your website, Telegram and other services
04·Tasks

What a CRM does for you

  • 01

    No client gets lost

    Enquiries from the website, the bot and the phone are collected in one database with their history.

  • 02

    Order in sales

    You can see who is doing what on each deal, and tasks are not forgotten.

  • 03

    Decisions based on numbers

    The owner sees request and sales reports at any moment.
