About

About Akmal — website and Telegram bot developer in Tashkent

My name is Akmal. I'm a developer based in Tashkent: I build websites, Telegram bots, Mini Apps and CRM systems for business and run every project myself — from discovery to launch and support. I work with small and mid-sized businesses and with companies that need their own system for managing clients, directly, without an agency or a web studio.

My job isn't just to ship a website or a bot — it's to make them useful: collecting requests, saving your team's time and helping you sell.

01·Why me

Why it’s easy to work with me

  • 01

    One person in charge

    You talk directly to the person doing the work. No account managers, no broken telephone, no agency markup.

  • 02

    Full cycle

    Discovery, design, development, launch and support — all in one pair of hands. No need to hire a designer, a developer and someone to host it separately.

  • 03

  • 04

02·How I work

How I work on your project

You know in advance what happens at each stage and what you get at the end of it.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    I get to know your business and processes: where requests come from and where time gets lost. We decide what's needed at launch.

    You getScope, timeline and price

  2. 02

    Design

    I plan the structure and interface: which sections, what the client sees and where they click. We sign off the layout before development starts.

    You getAn approved layout

  3. 03

    Development

    I build the working product from the layout: pages, forms, payments, accounts, and links to your CRM and Telegram.

    You getA working version to review

  4. 04

    Testing

    I check every scenario on phones and computers, fix bugs and make your edits within the agreed scope.

    You getA launch-ready version

  5. 05

    Launch

    I put the project on a server, connect your domain and a secure connection, and set up analytics.

    You getA live project on your domain

  6. 06

    Support

    After launch I stay in touch: updates, backups, improvements and new features as your business grows.

    You getStable operation and growth
