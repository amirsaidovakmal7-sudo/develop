Services · Telegram Mini App

Mini App Telegram Mini App development, end to end

I develop Telegram Mini Apps — full web apps that open right inside Telegram: nothing to install, no sign-up, in the messenger your clients already use. I build mini apps for any business task, from an online store in Telegram to an internal service for your team.

Discuss a project→
01·In short

What a Telegram Mini App is

It is an app inside Telegram: a client taps a button in a bot or a link in a channel, and an interface with a catalogue, a cart, a personal account and payment opens. Telegram already knows who the user is, so there are no passwords or login forms, and notifications arrive in the same chat. Everything a website can do works in the messenger.

02·Formats

Mini Apps I build

  • 01

    Store in Telegram

    A catalogue with search and filters, a cart, payment and order status — a store where your clients already talk.

  • 02

    Bookings and reservations

    Choosing a service, a specialist and a time, with booking reminders — for salons, clinics, schools and studios.

  • 03

    Personal account and loyalty

    Order history, bonuses, memberships and personal offers for regular clients.

  • 04

    Service for your team

    Internal tools: requests, tasks, reports and records right in your staff’s Telegram.
