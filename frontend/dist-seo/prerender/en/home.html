03·How I work

Six steps from brief to launch

You know in advance what happens at each stage and what you get at the end of it.

010203040506/ 06Discovery
  1. 01

    Discovery

    I get to know your business and processes: where requests come from and where time gets lost. We decide what's needed at launch.

    You getScope, timeline and price

  2. 02

    Design

    I plan the structure and interface: which sections, what the client sees and where they click. We sign off the layout before development starts.

    You getAn approved layout

  3. 03

    Development

    I build the working product from the layout: pages, forms, payments, accounts, and links to your CRM and Telegram.

    You getA working version to review

  4. 04

    Testing

    I check every scenario on phones and computers, fix bugs and make your edits within the agreed scope.

    You getA launch-ready version

  5. 05

    Launch

    I put the project on a server, connect your domain and a secure connection, and set up analytics.

    You getA live project on your domain

  6. 06

    Support

    After launch I stay in touch: updates, backups, improvements and new features as your business grows.

    You getStable operation and growth
